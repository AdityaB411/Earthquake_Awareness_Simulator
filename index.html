📋 Scenario: ▼

A sudden earthquake occurs in an office environment.

What Should You Do?

1. Drop to your hands and knees, and protect your head and neck.

2. If a sturdy desk or table is nearby, get underneath it and press [E] to take cover in the simulation.

3. Hold on to your cover and stay protected while shaking continues.

4. Be prepared for possible aftershocks.

5. When shaking stops, check for hazards and use the exit only when it is safe.

WARNING: Do not use the heater or electrical-panel area as a cover location in this simulation.

WARNING: Lightweight or unstable objects may topple.

📚 Key Takeaways:

  • Drop, Cover, and Hold On; use a sturdy desk or table when one is nearby.
  • Taking cover promptly can help reduce the risk of injury.

  • Watch for fallen objects and other hazards.
  • After shaking stops, check for hazards and exit carefully when safe.
